Waves 8–16

The next nine waves after wave 7, laid out by the rules we set. Each wave has its own Shark entrance, and every medium reacts to it. Dots mark where each fish is at that moment; lines show the whole route it is on.

  • One Shark per wave, entering from a new edge each time
  • Manta every other wave, meeting about 1 visit in 3
  • Mediums flow against the Shark
  • At most 2 fast fish crossing at once
  • Max 3 big fish on screen

Wave 8

Manta first, then a curious Shark

MantaHammerhead

The Manta glides the top row with its followers while the Hammerhead patrols the bottom seats. The Shark waits until the Manta is gone, then drops in from the top, inspects a bottom seat and climbs back out.

Shark: Inspector (Curious) · 15 mediums enter · This Shark route has no left-right direction, so mediums keep to their own side instead.

Wave 9

The Shark cuts a diagonal

HammerheadMarlin ×2

A Marlin opens the wave with a fast dash. The Shark comes in from the bottom-left corner and climbs to the top-right, so the mediums split to the two corners it leaves open. After it passes, the Hammerhead turns down the right side and a second Marlin slashes through a Sardine school.

Shark: Diagonal (King) · 15 mediums enter · While the Shark was crossing, 3 medium crossings went against it and 0 with it.

Wave 10

Meeting: Shark and Manta

Meeting

Meeting wave. The Manta glides the bottom seat row right to left. The Shark gate only allows a calm lane far from it, so the Shark cruises the top row, entering from the edge farthest from the Manta. Mediums stay in the middle band between the two. No Hammerhead or Marlin during the meeting.

Shark: Cruise (King) · 16 mediums enter · While the Shark was crossing, 4 medium crossings went against it and 0 with it.

Wave 11

An aggressive Charge

Hammerhead

Short and sharp. A Marlin hooks in and out, then an aggressive Shark charges straight across the middle, right to left, in seven seconds. The Hammerhead follows by sweeping the top row.

Shark: Charge (Aggressive) · 14 mediums enter · While the Shark was crossing, 2 medium crossings went against it and 0 with it.

Wave 12

Feeding circle, then the Manta

MantaHammerhead

A hungry Shark comes in from the right and circles on the right side. The Hammerhead keeps to the left with a side swing. Once the Shark leaves, a Marlin dashes through and the Manta glides corner to corner with its followers.

Shark: Feeding circle (Hungry) · 14 mediums enter · This Shark route has no left-right direction, so mediums keep to their own side instead.

Wave 13

Two Sharks

2 SharksHammerhead pairMarlin ×2

Rare double. The Shark patrols the left side and a smaller Shark shadows it 2.5 seconds behind in the same lane. The right half belongs to the mediums. Once both Sharks leave, two Hammerheads patrol the top and bottom seat rows, mirrored.

Shark: Patrol loop (King) · 16 mediums enter · This Shark route has no left-right direction, so mediums keep to their own side instead.

Wave 14

Manta loop, then a Stalker

MantaHammerhead

The Manta circles on the left side while the Hammerhead runs the top seats the other way. When the Manta leaves, a Marlin dashes through, then a curious Shark stalks slowly from right to left through the lower middle.

Shark: Stalker (Curious) · 16 mediums enter · While the Shark was crossing, 3 medium crossings went against it and 0 with it.

Wave 15

The Shark splits the screen

The Shark drops straight down the middle. Nothing crosses the center while it is there: each half gets its own fish that come in and leave on the same side. A Marlin crosses 4 seconds after the Shark is gone.

Shark: Split (King) · 13 mediums enter · This Shark route has no left-right direction, so mediums keep to their own side instead.

Wave 16

Meeting: the Shark has to wait

MeetingShark waits

Meeting wave on a Grand arc. The Manta covers most of the screen, so no Shark lane fits and the Shark waits off screen. It enters at 31 s along the top row once the Manta has passed. This happens about once every 36 waves.

Shark: Cruise (King) · 16 mediums enter · While the Shark was crossing, 2 medium crossings went against it and 1 with it.

Seats are the short marks on the top and bottom edges. Shaded columns on each timeline show when the Shark is on screen. The Shark's buffer lane is the soft orange band. Movement never changes catch odds or pays.

Fish Route Library

Every fish's routes and what each one is for, plus the laws the director follows. The pictures show the screen with seats on the top and bottom edges; fish enter and leave off screen. In play, any route can also be flipped or run backwards.

Movement never changes catch chances or pays. Routes only decide where and when fish swim.

Shark

big · pays 100-400xin game (v6)
Role
Apex. Sets the plan every wave; everyone else reads its lane and reacts.
Home
Its own lane plus a buffer band; changes every wave
Habit
One per wave. Never enters from the same edge or travels the same direction two waves in a row. Picks an attitude, then a route from that attitude.

Reactions. Snap back: Reverses along its own lane at 1.6x speed and leaves where it came in. (14 hits in 3 s, 50%) Frenzy: 3 s of 1.5x speed with a hard side-to-side shake. (14 hits in 3 s, 50%)

Second Shark moves. Shadow (in game), Pincer (in game), Hunting pair (designed), Size-up (designed), Food fight (designed), Standoff (designed), Waiting holder (designed), Frenzy spreads (designed)

  • Cruise 18–22 s
    King

    The calm default: owns one horizontal lane at one of four heights. Allowed during Manta meetings.

  • Patrol loop 20–26 s
    King

    Loops one side and leaves the way it came, handing the other half to the mediums. Allowed during meetings.

  • Deep sweep 20–24 s
    King

    Sweeps a seat row; pushes the Octopus to the other seat row. Allowed during meetings.

  • Center line 22–26 s
    King

    Straight through the middle; splits the screen into top and bottom halves. Allowed during meetings.

  • Split 12–16 s
    King

    Top to bottom through the center. Nothing crosses the middle while it is there; each half keeps its own fish.

  • Diagonal 18–22 s
    King

    Corner to corner; mediums take the two corners it leaves open.

  • Strike 9–12 s
    Hungry

    A short fast lunge through the middle.

  • Feeding circle 22–28 s
    Hungry

    Circles one spot, then leaves the way it came. The only Hungry route allowed in meetings, on the far side.

  • Stalker 24–30 s
    Curious

    Slow wavy pass through the lower middle: a long, easy target.

  • Inspector 20–26 s
    Curious

    Drops in to look at one seat and climbs back out; that seat feels watched.

  • Bubble check 22–26 s
    Curious

    Lingers near the center with small turns.

  • Charge 6–8 s
    Aggressive

    Fast straight run across in 6-8 s: a jolt.

  • Thrash 12–15 s
    Aggressive

    Fast zigzag; looks angry and is hard to track.

  • Rush 7–9 s
    Aggressive

    Fast corner-to-corner run.

Manta Ray

big · pays 150-300xin game (v6)
Role
Second respect point, the attractor. Brings 8-12 small followers that tighten when a Shark is near.
Home
Half the screen; never the Shark lane
Habit
Every other wave. Meets the Shark about 1 visit in 3 (only through the Shark gate). Never flees, never speeds past 1.3x.
  • Grand arc 30–35 s

    Covers most of the screen. In a meeting no Shark lane fits, so the Shark waits off screen.

  • Open glide 30–35 s

    Straight glide in the top or bottom half; leaves the other half free.

  • Slow S 30–35 s

    Middle-to-low S; only the far edge is left for a meeting Shark.

  • Feeding loop 30–35 s

    Circles one side; followers bunch around it.

  • Seat row glide 30–35 s

    Glides along a seat row; that row gets the followers up close.

  • Wide diagonal 30–35 s

    Corner to corner glide.

Hammerhead

big · pays 80-150xin game (v6)
Role
Edge guard. Gives way to the Shark and the Manta.
Home
Seat rows and sides
Habit
2 waves in 3; 1 in 6 of those is a pair patrol on both seat rows. Takes the edge opposite the Shark.
  • Rail patrol 20–24 s

    Patrols one seat row with a light weave. Also used for the pair patrol, mirrored on both rows.

  • Corner turn 18–22 s

    Top row, then down a side.

  • Head sweep 20–24 s

    Sways side to side along a row.

  • Wide U 22–26 s

    In and out on the same side.

  • Low arc 18–22 s
    no Shark on screen

    Under the middle. Only when no Shark is on screen.

  • Side swing 12–16 s

    A short visit to one side.

Marlin

big · pays 150-500xin game (v6)
Role
Speed trophy. Only dashes into clear water; crosses a Shark lane only 2+ s after it passes.
Home
Clear water, a few seconds at a time
Habit
Every wave, twice in about 1 wave in 4. Never during a Shark + Manta meeting. One at a time. Counts as a fast crosser.
  • Dash 5–7 s

    Fast straight lane at one of five heights.

  • Diagonal dash 6–8 s

    Corner to corner.

  • Hook back 7–9 s

    Dashes in, snaps back out the same side.

  • Sharp cut 6–8 s

    One hard angle.

  • Drift, then burst 9–12 s

    Slow entry, then a fast exit.

  • School slash 6–8 s
    aims at a real Sardine or Anchovy school

    Cuts through a school, which scatters and regroups.

Tuna

medium · pays 8-15xdesigned, not in game yet (game v7 has the older medium set)
Role
Cheapest medium, most predictable. Easy team shooting.
Home
Corners
Habit
Schools of 2-4 that hold formation (side-by-side arc or diagonal echelon). Always enters and leaves at corners.
  • Corner run 12–15 s
    crosser

    Corner to opposite corner as a block.

  • Crisscross 12–15 s
    counts as 2 crossers

    Two schools at once, mirrored, crossing in an X. Best used as the release right after a Shark leaves.

  • Corner swing 12–15 s
    crosser

    Corner to the neighbouring corner in an arch; flips to open up or down away from the Shark.

  • Corner hook 12–15 s

    Stays on one side, corner to corner. The Tuna option while a Shark splits the screen.

Barracuda

medium · pays 10-25xdesigned, not in game yet (game v7 has the older medium set)
Role
Hunter. Its pauses are the moments to shoot it.
Home
Middle band
Habit
Only straight lines and sharp corners; pauses at every corner. Waits at its ambush spot until a Shark has passed.
  • Ambush 14–18 s
    crosser1 pause

    Creeps in, holds still, then darts out to a corner.

  • Zigzag stalk 16–20 s
    crosser4 pauses

    Straight legs with a stop at every corner.

  • Strike and retreat 14–18 s
    1 pause

    Darts in, stops, backs out the side it came from. Never crosses the middle.

Octopus

medium · pays 12-30xdesigned, not in game yet (game v7 has the older medium set)
Role
Personal fish: hangs out at a seat.
Home
Seat edges only, never the middle
Habit
Moves to the opposite seat row when a Shark takes its row. 7 hits in 3 s: squirts ink and jets for the nearest edge (show only).
  • Seat crawl 17–22 s
    2 pauses

    Crawls along a seat row and rests twice.

  • Edge hops 17–22 s

    Hops along the edge in bursts.

  • Rise and sink 17–22 s
    1 pause

    Comes up from under a seat, rests, sinks back.

medium · pays 15-35xdesigned, not in game yet (game v7 has the older medium set)
Role
Bully: fast, risky, top medium pay.
Home
Anywhere, fast
Habit
Straight lines with at most one hard angle. The only medium that goes top to bottom.
  • Spear 8–11 s
    crosser

    Drops straight down (or up) through the screen.

  • Ricochet 8–11 s
    crosser

    Bounces off a seat row like a pool shot.

  • Low slash 8–11 s
    crosser

Sea Turtle

medium · pays 20-50xdesigned, not in game yet (game v7 has the older medium set)
Role
Calm: easy to catch but pays the most of the mediums. May drift near the Manta.
Home
Center
Habit
Slow curves only; the only fish that circles. Not counted as a fast crosser.
  • Lazy S 22–27 s

    A slow S through the center.

  • Slow circle 22–27 s

    One lazy loop in the middle.

  • Seat visit 22–27 s
    1 pause

    Dips to a seat and rests there.

  • Side loop 22–27 s

    Loops on one side. The Turtle option while a Shark splits the screen.

Sardine

tiny · pays 2-3xin game (v5)
Role
The crowd. Fills the screen and hits every corner.
Home
Everywhere; a coverage map sends schools to the emptiest zones
Habit
Schools of 6-9; 35% of the time a mirror twin school takes the reflected route. 1 in 700 hits is a Golden Sardine (50x).
  • Perimeter by speed

    A lap around the whole table past every seat.

  • Corner S by speed

    Corner to corner in an S.

  • W dip by speed

    Dips to the bottom seats three times.

  • Figure 8 by speed

    A figure eight through the middle.

  • Seat sweep by speed

    Along one seat row, around, back along the other.

  • Corner arc by speed

    Up from a bottom corner and across the top.

Angelfish

small · pays 3-8xin game (v5)
Role
The messenger. Crosses alone; freezes near the Shark, drifts to the Manta.
Home
Anywhere, alone
Habit
Solo; crosses the screen in 25-35 s with a gentle wobble.
  • Diagonal down 25–35 s

    Slow diagonal, top-left to bottom-right.

  • Diagonal up 25–35 s

    Slow diagonal, bottom-left to top-right.

  • Steep 25–35 s

    Steep drop from the top.

  • Vertical 25–35 s

    Straight down one side.

  • Hook 25–35 s

    Drops in and hooks out the side.

  • Arch 25–35 s

    Rises from the bottom and sinks back.

Not designed yet

  • Small fish (Clownfish, Butterflyfish, Puffer, Lionfish, Seahorse, Minnow, Anchovy): not designed yet; drift at random in the game
  • Specials (Bomb Crab, Lightning Eel): not designed yet; drift at random in the game
  • Bosses (Megalodon, Kraken, Giant Squid, Healer Jellyfish): not designed yet; use the old stay-on-screen movement

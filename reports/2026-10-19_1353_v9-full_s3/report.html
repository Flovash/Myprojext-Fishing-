The player types

Sniper
Big fish and bosses only, lock-on, bet 10. Holds fire otherwise. Sits down with 2,000, goes home after 60 min.
Sprayer
Fires as fast as the gun allows at the closest fish, bet 1. Sits down with 1,000, goes home after 60 min.
Chaser
Starts at bet 2, doubles after every 12 shots without a catch (cap 100), back to 2 after a catch. Sits down with 2,000, goes home after 60 min.
Grinder
Steady bet 2, aims at mediums first, then smalls. Sits down with 1,000, goes home after 90 min.
Whale
Bet 100 on bosses and Sharks, 50 on other big fish (lock-on), 10 on anything else. Sits down with 20,000, goes home after 60 min.
Fisher
About 85% of the time on the pole with base and medium baits; lands 90% of fights. Gun at bet 3 otherwise. Market: holds fish for the season-end bonus, sells only when short of points. Sits down with 2,000, goes home after 90 min.
Casual
Bets 1-5 on random fish, takes pauses, sometimes fishes. Leaves when down 400. Market: sells the bag once it holds 3 fish. Sits down with 1,000, goes home after 45 min.
Newbie
Slow aim, fires before lined up (misses a lot), slow fire, bets 1-3. Lands 60% of fights. Market: sells every fish right away. Sits down with 500, goes home after 45 min.

A player leaves when they bust, quit (Casual only) or hit their time limit; the seat stays empty 10–30 s, then the next type in the rotation sits down.

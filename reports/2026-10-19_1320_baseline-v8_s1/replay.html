        Peacock Reef

        A fish table for 1 to 10 players. Shoot with the gun, or switch to the fishing pole and cast bait. Every shot costs your bet and a catch pays the fish's multiplier times your bet. Every fish pays a range, and everyone plays the same odds. The ring around a fish shows its group. Each wave the ocean changes scene, and some waves end with a boss.

        JoystickLeft / right aims. Double push forward locks on. Button 1 fires or casts. Button 2 sets the bet or bait. Button 3 switches gun / pole.

          Press any button on a connected controller to start. Add credits in Settings, or press a seat's number key (1 to 0) during play.

          Settings

          Seats and credits

          On Auto, seat 1 uses the first controller, seat 2 the second, and so on. Seats 1 to 3 also take the keyboard, and seat 1 takes the mouse or touch. Add puts credits on that seat. During play, number keys 1 to 0 do the same for seats 1 to 10.

          Controllers

          Plug in USB arcade encoders or gamepads, then press a button on each one so the browser can see it. Lit numbers show which buttons are pressed.

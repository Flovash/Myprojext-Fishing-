# Plan: Rituals

Status: idea stage (2026-10-18). Nothing built yet. Pick the rituals you like, change anything, and Claude Code turns the chosen ones into route plans.

A **ritual** is a moment when the whole ocean reacts to one fish or one event. The sardines start circling and every other school joins in; the Megalodon comes through and everything hugs the edges. Today each fish swims its own routes. Rituals connect them.

**Law zero still holds.** Rituals change where and when fish swim, and how the water looks. They never change catch chances or pays. A fish in a ritual pays exactly what it pays outside one.

---

## 1. What each fish does that others could react to

These are the "signals" in our ocean: things one fish does that give every other fish a reason to change course. Built from each fish's habit in the library.

| Fish | What it does (signal) | How other fish could react |
|---|---|---|
| **Sardine** | Circles in a tight ball (figure8, perimeter) | Other small schools **join** the circle; predators **orbit** outside it |
| **Anchovy** | Bait ball; rides in a big fish's wake | Join the sardine ball; tag behind Manta and Turtle |
| **Tuna** | Corner runs in formation | **Herd**: push small schools toward the middle so they ball up |
| **Marlin** | Fast dash and slash | Cuts **through** a bait ball; schools split around it and rejoin |
| **Shark** | One pass per wave, picks an attitude | Smalls **scatter**; Barracuda waits for it to pass; Octopus switches rows; Hammerhead takes the opposite edge (already in game) |
| **Hammerhead** | Patrols a seat row (rail) | Schools **stay off** its row while it's there |
| **Manta** | Big slow arcs, feeding loop | Small fish **follow its wake**; it can host a **cleaning station** |
| **Turtle** | The only fish that circles | **Carousel**: clownfish and butterflies circle with it |
| **Megalodon** | Never stops, laps every seat row | **Everything clears**: schools hug the edges and the corners until it passes |
| **Kraken** | Barely moves; tentacles sweep the seat rows | Fish **leave the seat rows** and swim the middle lane |
| **Giant Squid** | Jets backwards, squirts ink | **Ink cloud**: fish swerve around the dark patch |
| **Healer Jellyfish** | Pulses up, trails baby jellies | **Shelter**: small fish hide among its trail, calm and slow |
| **Lightning Eel** | Glows brighter near other fish | **Glow night**: fish drift toward the light |
| **Barracuda** | Sits still at an ambush spot | Small fish **skirt around** it in a wide curve |
| **Swordfish** | The only medium going top to bottom | **Parts the water**: schools split left and right like a gate |
| **Octopus** | Crawls a seat row; inks and jets when hit hard | Smalls **scatter** from the ink; the crawl row is left to it |
| **Lionfish** | Escorts big fish; fans its fins | **Escort**: rides beside the big fish's route |
| **Seahorse** | Pair dance | **Courtship**: butterfly pairs and seahorse pairs dance together |
| **Butterflyfish** | Pairs fluttering inside a group | Joins the courtship dance and the Turtle carousel |

| **Pufferfish** | Puffs up when hit hard | A small **keep-away bubble** others go around |
| **Minnow** | Zigzags with sudden stops | **Seesaw** with sardines (already in game); joins the scatter |
| **Bomb Crab** | Walks sideways into a crowd | The crowd **gathers** first; the crab follows the crowd |
| **Angelfish** | Solo diagonal with a wobble | Joins the Turtle carousel or the migration lane |

## 2. Ritual ideas from the real ocean

Each one: what really happens, who **leads**, what everyone else does, and how it would look on our table.

### A. Bait Ball (Sardine Circle), the one you described
- **Real ocean:** when predators come, sardines pack into a spinning ball. Tuna, dolphins, sharks and marlin circle it and take turns dashing in.
- **Leader:** Sardine (two schools merge into one big circle in the middle of the table).
- **Joins the circle:** Anchovy, Minnow, Butterfly (tiny and small schools).
- **Orbits outside:** Tuna (herding laps), Barracuda (still, waiting), Shark (slow circle).
- **Dashes through:** Marlin and Swordfish, one pass each; the ball splits and re-forms.
- **Ends:** the ball bursts and every school scatters toward the seat rows.

### B. Sardine Run (Great Migration)
- **Real ocean:** every winter, billions of sardines run up the South African coast, followed by everything that eats them.
- **Leader:** a long river of sardine and anchovy schools, all crossing the same way in one lane.
- **Everyone else:** follows the river in the same direction (tuna on the edges, sharks and marlin behind). Nothing swims against it.
- **On our table:** a 20-30 s conveyor belt across the table, passing every seat.

### C. Cleaning Station
- **Real ocean:** big fish (mantas, turtles, sharks) line up and hold still at a reef spot while small cleaner fish pick them clean. Even predators don't hunt there.
- **Leader:** Manta or Turtle stops near the middle.
- **Joins:** Clownfish, Butterfly and Angelfish circle close around it.
- **Everyone else:** predators pass by slowly and don't chase; schools give the station room.

### D. Coral Spawning Night
- **Real ocean:** a few nights a year, after a full moon, the whole reef releases eggs at once, a glowing underwater snowstorm. Every small fish comes up to feed.
- **On our table:** the water darkens a little and tiny glowing specks rise (show only). Every tiny and small school drifts slowly toward the middle and swirls. Lightning Eel and Healer Jellyfish glow.
- **Fits with:** seasons (it could be the end-of-season ritual).

### E. Make Way (Royal Passage)
- **Real ocean:** when a huge predator moves through, everything else gets out of the way.
- **Leader:** Megalodon, Kraken or Giant Squid entering.
- **Everyone else:** clears the lane. Schools hug the corners and edges, big fish wait, and nothing crosses its path until it settles. The Kraken version empties the seat rows and moves everything to the middle.
- **Mostly built already:** fish scatter from the Shark and Megalodon today. This makes it table-wide and choreographed.

### F. Current / Tide
- **Real ocean:** currents carry everything; mantas and turtles ride them, small fish swim with them.
- **On our table:** for a while the whole ocean drifts one way. Every route bends with the current, Manta and Turtle glide faster with it, and small schools swim with it, never against it.
- **Changes direction** each time, so every seat row gets its turn.

### G. Courtship Dance
- **Real ocean:** seahorses dance at dawn, pairs tail-linked, changing color.
- **Leader:** Seahorse pairs.
- **Joins:** butterfly pairs pair-dance nearby; Clownfish stay home at their seats.
- **Everyone else:** calm, slow routes; no fast crossers.

### H. Feeding Frenzy (Shark Hunt)
- **Real ocean:** when one predator starts feeding, others rush in.
- **Leader:** Shark with the hungry or aggressive attitude.
- **Joins:** Hammerhead, Barracuda and Marlin speed up and cut across; small fish flash-scatter in waves across the table.
- **Short and loud:** 10-15 s, then calm.

### I. Glow Night (Bioluminescence)
- **Real ocean:** at night, plankton light up when anything moves, so every fish leaves a glowing trail.
- **On our table:** dim water, every fish leaves a faint light trail (show only). The Eel is brightest; small fish gather around it.

### J. Ink Storm
- **Real ocean:** squid and octopus fill the water with ink to escape.
- **Leader:** Giant Squid or a hard-hit Octopus.
- **Everyone else:** steers around the cloud; small fish exit the dark patch the short way.

## 3. Respecting the rituals: how the routes get organized

You said it: if one fish starts a ritual, **every** fish needs a plan for it. Otherwise someone swims straight through the bait ball and the ritual looks broken. So every fish gets a **role** in every ritual, and each role has a route.

### Roles (the same short list for every ritual)

| Role | What the fish does |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Starts and shapes the ritual (the sardines in a Bait Ball) |
| **Join** | Becomes part of it (anchovies joining the circle) |
| **Orbit** | Circles around it at a distance (tuna herding the ball) |
| **Strike** | One pass through it, then leaves (marlin) |
| **Escort** | Rides alongside the leader (lionfish) |
| **Clear** | Gets out of the way: edges, corners or off-screen |
| **Shelter** | Hides near a safe spot (the Healer's trail, its own seat) |
| **Hold** | Keeps doing its own thing, but stays out of the ritual's space |

### The ritual table (who does what)
One row per fish, one column per ritual, a role in every cell. **No empty cells.** That is how we know every fish respects every ritual. Example for the Bait Ball:

| Fish | Bait Ball role | Route for it |
|---|---|---|
| Sardine | Lead | ballSpin (new) |
| Anchovy, Minnow, Butterfly | Join | ballJoin (new: spiral into the ball) |
| Tuna | Orbit | herdLap (new) |
| Barracuda | Orbit | ambush (existing, spot set at the ball's edge) |
| Shark | Orbit | slow circle (new attitude route) |
| Marlin, Swordfish | Strike | slash, spear (existing, aimed through the ball) |
| Turtle, Manta | Hold | their own routes, kept outside the ball's ring |
| Octopus, Clownfish | Shelter | stay on their seat rows |
| Megalodon, Kraken, Giant Squid | Not sent | bosses don't appear during a Bait Ball |

### Where it lives in the route library
```
route_library/director/rituals/
    bait_ball.json        what starts it, how long, how often, the space it takes, the role of every fish
    sardine_run.json
    ...
route_library/fish/<fish>/rituals/
    bait_ball.json        this fish's role and its route during the ritual
```
- `build_library.js` checks that **every fish has a role in every ritual** and refuses to build if one is missing. That enforces "respect the rituals".
- ROUTES.md and the Reef Playbook get a Rituals section, with one sketch per ritual showing everyone's paths at once.
- The lab tracks every ritual like a play: when it happened, who was there, and how the table did during it. The replay's **Jump to** list will show rituals.

### Rules every ritual follows
1. Law zero: no change to catch chances or pays.
2. It passes every seat. A ritual that only happens near one seat row has to rotate, like the Current changing direction.
3. It has a clear start, middle and end, and a maximum length (most 15-40 s).
4. Bosses and rituals don't overlap unless the ritual is the boss's own (Make Way).
5. One ritual at a time, with a cooldown between them.
6. Fast crosser and lane laws still apply, with exceptions written into the ritual file (the Marlin's Strike pass through a Bait Ball).

## 4. Decisions for you
1. **Which rituals?** Suggested first three: **Bait Ball** (your idea), **Make Way** (mostly built already) and **Current** (simple, and touches every fish).
2. **How often?** For example one ritual every 2-3 big-fish waves, or tied to seasons (each season has its own ritual).
3. **Do players get a warning?** For example a banner ("The sardines are gathering…") a few seconds before.
4. **Show-only effects:** the glow for Coral Spawning and Glow Night, and the ink cloud. They don't change play; are they wanted?

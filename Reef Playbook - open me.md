# Reef Playbook

What's in it:
- Fish: all 25 fish with the game's own icons. Tap a fish for its own page: attitude, routes, a live screen, and the rules each route follows.
- Master strategy (view only): waves 1-10 on one timeline, about 12 minutes, with law checks per wave.

The master strategy is also in this folder as a sheet:
"Reef Playbook - Master Strategy (waves 1-10)" - one row per fish visit (wave, when it comes in, how long it stays, fish, route).
